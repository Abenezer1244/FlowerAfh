[ ABOUT US ]

Redefining care
with heart and home.

Flower Adult Family Home was founded on a simple premise: care should feel like family. We've built an environment where dignity, profound comfort, and professional support seamlessly intertwine to create a true sanctuary for your loved ones.

Our Purpose

To provide unwavering support and enrich daily living.

Our mission is to deliver deeply personalized, compassionate care that honors the unique history and individuality of each resident. We exist to alleviate the physical and emotional burdens of aging and recovery, replacing them with a nurturing atmosphere where residents feel genuinely valued, unconditionally safe, and at peace. Every action we take is guided by empathy and professional excellence.

Our Vision

Setting a new standard for adult family care.

We envision a community where institutional, impersonal care is entirely replaced by warm, residential sanctuaries. A future where families never have to compromise between top-tier medical assistance and a comfortable, homelike environment. We are continuously striving to be the benchmark for adult family homes, recognized for our innovative care models and profound dedication to human dignity.

[ THE HISTORY ]

Our Journey

2018 • The Inception

Recognizing the Need

After witnessing the impersonal nature of large-scale facilities, our founders realized the pressing need for a more intimate care model. The blueprint for Flower Adult Family Home was drafted, focusing intensely on low resident-to-staff ratios and the preservation of homelike comfort.

2020 • Opening Doors

A Home Realized

We officially opened our doors, welcoming our first residents into a fully renovated, highly accessible, and beautifully designed residential space. Our core team of certified caregivers was established, setting the unbreakable foundation for our daily care philosophy.

2023 • Expansion of Care

Specialized Services

To better serve our growing community, we expanded our expertise to include dedicated memory care programs and short-term respite stays. We integrated advanced health monitoring technology while fiercely preserving the warmth of our traditional home environment.
